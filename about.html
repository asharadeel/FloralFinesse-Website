About Me

I launched my business in February of 2024 at 17 years old. It started as an idea and 2 days later i made it a reality. I knew the idea was put into my head for a reason. Roses have now developed a much deeper meaning for me, one I would have never anticipated.

It brings me so much pleasure to help my customers in selecting the perfect bouquet. I take pride in helping people express emotions that words may not be able to capture, as somebody whose not the best with wording how I feel. There's something truly special about witnessing the warmth and happiness that arises when someone receives a timeless symbol of love and appreciation.
